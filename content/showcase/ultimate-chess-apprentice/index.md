Step into a welcoming 3D chess experience where every match is a chance to sharpen your thinking. Developed by Ultimate Games Studios, Ultimate Chess Apprentice brings together classic chess, characterful computer opponents, and a career ladder that gives you a reason to return to the board.

Whether you are finding your feet, practicing familiar strategies, or looking for a tougher challenge, choose your level and play at your own pace.

Build your chess career

Create your player profile, choose an avatar, and begin your journey through a ladder of opponents. Win matches to advance through the ranks, progressing from Beginner and Apprentice to Intermediate, Advanced, Expert, and Master.

Your career progress is saved, and an unfinished career match can be continued later—so you can take your time over the next move.

Meet opponents with personality

Computer opponents bring expressive portraits and reactions to the match. They respond to checks, captures, and clever moves with dialogue that adds personality to each encounter.

Six difficulty levels let you choose between a gentler introduction and more demanding tactical play. Experiment with different approaches as you learn to recognise threats, protect your pieces, and turn opportunities into advantages.
