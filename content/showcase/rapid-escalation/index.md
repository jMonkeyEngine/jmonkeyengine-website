Rapid Escalation is a fast-paced incremental space arena game built around short, explosive battles and permanent progression.

Start with a simple ship and survive quick combat encounters to earn cash. Between battles, reinvest your earnings into upgrades for firepower, speed, accuracy, shields, battle duration, and enemy intensity.

Each upgrade permanently reshapes your build, pushing the next run into faster, deadlier, and more profitable territory as your ship grows from a fragile starter craft into an unstoppable war machine.
