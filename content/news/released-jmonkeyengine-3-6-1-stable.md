<div>A new patch release of JMonkeyEngine arrived on Friday: 3.6.1-stable. This is now the recommended version for all JME projects.  Among other things, it solves issues with instancing, memory management, and restarting LWJGLv2 contexts. The release notes are online at <a href="https://github.com/jMonkeyEngine/jmonkeyengine/releases/tag/v3.6.1-stable">https://github.com/jMonkeyEngine/jmonkeyengine/releases/tag/v3.6.1-stable</a><br /><br />Our Engine continues to look to the future and evolve to meet it. For instance, there are efforts underway to support multiple monitors and to leverage OpenXR for virtual-reality applications.<br /><br />Thank you for your financial contribution(s) to the JMonkeyEngine project. With your support, the project remains in good financial health. If you'd like to connect with us and/or learn more about our Engine, the best resource is probably our Discourse forum at <a href="https://opencollective.com/redirect?url=https%3A%2F%2Fhub.jmonkeyengine.org">https://hub.jmonkeyengine.org</a> </div>
