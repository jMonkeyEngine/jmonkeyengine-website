News from JMonkeyEngine - 21 April 2022

A new release of JMonkeyEngine arrived today: 3.5.2-stable. Among other things, it improves our support for armhf platforms --- such as Raspberry Pi single-board computers. The release notes are online at https://github.com/jMonkeyEngine/jmonkeyengine/releases/tag/v3.5.2-stable

Engine development continues on many other fronts, including buffer objects and the Android application lifecycle.

Thank you for your financial contribution(s) to the JMonkeyEngine project. If you'd like to connect with us and/or learn more about our Engine, one excellent resource is our Discourse forum at https://hub.jmonkeyengine.org

The modern game engine for Java developers. Open source, BSD-3 licensed, since 2004.
