Choose where your donation goes

jMonkeyEngine accepts donations through OpenCollective. If you donate to the main fund, the Core Developers decide how to allocate the money across development, maintenance, releases, hosting, and community services.

You can also target a dedicated project fund if you want your contribution reserved for a specific purpose.

Backers can leave a message to the community that may be featured on the homepage.
